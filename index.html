Our Story

From a Diwali market spark to a burning dream.

I'm Deepanshi Verma — after 9 years in corporate, I asked myself one quiet question: "If I can build someone else's dream, why not my own?"

A simple Diwali shopping day planted the first seed. And on 7th November 2025, I poured my first candle — the Golden Bloom. One week later, on 14th November 2025, Deep by Soni Aura Candles was born.

Every candle you hold carries dreams, craftsmanship, and the soul of an artist. This brand is not just about candles — it is about emotions, moments, and memories.

Deepanshi Verma Founder & Candle Artist
