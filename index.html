Our Story

The Journey of Deepanshi

From 9 years in corporate to becoming a candle artist — a story of courage, family, and following your heart.

D

"If I can build someone else's dream, why not my own?"

I spent 9 years in the corporate world — worked hard, delivered my best, and helped build other people's dreams. Until one day, I quietly asked myself that question.

I have always loved drawing, painting, cooking, and creating things with my own hands. Creativity felt like home. But I never imagined that this love would one day lead me to candle-making.

The real turning point came on a simple Diwali shopping day. I saw people happily picking up plain white candles — such small candles, yet so much joy. That's when the thought struck: If people love these simple candles so much, why not create something more beautiful, more unique?

On 7th November 2025, I poured my very first candle — the Golden Bloom. Exactly one week later, on 14th November 2025, Deep By Soni Aura Candles was officially launched.

Today, this brand is not just my work — it is my journey of courage, creativity, devotion, and turning light into art.

"Dreams don't need to be perfect — they just need a brave beginning."

Deepanshi VermaFounder & Candle Artist

Safe Packaging Guarantee

All our candles are handcrafted. Slight variations in color, size, and finish are natural and part of the charm — not a defect.

Fragile Product Notice

Candles are fragile products. We take utmost care in packaging, but any damage caused by mishandling or rough transportation must be reported within 48 hours of delivery with photos.

No Returns Policy

Due to the fragile nature of our handmade candles, we do not accept returns or exchanges. However, if your product arrives damaged, contact us within 48 hours with photos and we will make it right.

Orders are confirmed via WhatsApp or email after payment. Payment must be completed before dispatch.

Use & Safety

  • Trim the wick to ¼ inch before each use
  • Do not burn for more than 2-4 hours at a time
  • Keep away from children, pets, and flammable items
  • Always burn on a heat-resistant surface
